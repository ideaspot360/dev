  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p class="post-meta" style="margin-bottom: 24px;">
    <span>Last Updated: November 03, 2026</span> &bull; <span>Effective Date: November 03, 2026</span>
  </p>

  <p>At <strong>IdeaSpot360 Dev</strong> (accessible from <a href="https://dev.ideaspot360.com">https://dev.ideaspot360.com</a>), the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by IdeaSpot360 Dev and how it is used.</p>

  <h2>1. Log Files and Web Analytics</h2>
  <p>Like many other technical publications, IdeaSpot360 Dev makes use of standard server log files and privacy-focused analytics. The information inside the log files includes:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Internet Protocol (IP) addresses</li>
    <li>Browser type and version (User Agent)</li>
    <li>Internet Service Provider (ISP)</li>
    <li>Date and time stamp</li>
    <li>Referring and exit pages</li>
    <li>Approximate geographic region (country level)</li>
  </ul>
  <p>This data is analyzed exclusively in the aggregate to troubleshoot network performance, monitor DDoS attempts, analyze reader navigation trends, and optimize infrastructure. IP addresses are not linked to personally identifiable information.</p>

  <h2>2. Cookies, Web Beacons, and Advertising Partners</h2>
  <p>IdeaSpot360 Dev may use cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, to record user-specific information on which pages the site visitor accesses or visits, and to customize web page content based upon visitors' browser type.</p>

  <h3>Google AdSense and DoubleClick DART Cookies</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on <code>dev.ideaspot360.com</code>.</li>
    <li>Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.</li>
    <li>Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy</a>.</li>
  </ul>

  <p>Our third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on IdeaSpot360 Dev. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Other technologies (such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons) may also be used by our third-party ad networks to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on the site.</p>
  <p><em>Please note: IdeaSpot360 Dev has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.</em></p>

  <h2>3. Third-Party Privacy Policies</h2>
  <p>IdeaSpot360 Dev's Privacy Policy does not apply to, and we cannot control the activities of, other advertisers or websites. You should consult the respective privacy policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information on their practices as well as for instructions about how to opt-out of certain practices.</p>
  <p>You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.</p>

  <h2>4. GDPR and CCPA / CPRA Compliance</h2>
  <p>We respect your privacy rights under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Right to Know:</strong> You may request disclosure of the specific pieces of personal data collected.</li>
    <li><strong>Right to Deletion:</strong> You may request the deletion of any personal data collected from you.</li>
    <li><strong>Right to Non-Discrimination:</strong> We do not discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights.</li>
    <li><strong>Sale of Personal Information:</strong> We do not sell personal data to any third parties.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>5. Children's Information</h2>
  <p>IdeaSpot360 Dev does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that IdeaSpot360 Dev has in its database the personal information of a child under the age of 13, please contact us immediately and we will use our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.</p>
